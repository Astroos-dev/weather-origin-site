Support & Documentation

Frequently asked questions, permissions guidance, and support contact for Weather Origin.

What is Weather Origin?

Weather Origin is a specialized meteorological and astronomical intelligence application. Unlike standard weather apps that focus only on surface conditions, Weather Origin provides deep atmospheric data designed for night sky observation, astrophotography, stargazing, and daytime planning.

Permissions Explained

  • Location Permission: Used only when you tap the GPS button to fetch weather and astronomical data for your exact location. You can alternatively search for any city worldwide without granting location permission.
  • Notification Permission (Android 13+): Used solely if you opt in to the Daily Weather Briefings (Morning summary, Midday shifts, and Night outlook). Notifications are processed on-device without background battery drain.

Unit System Settings

Weather Origin supports three unit modes accessible via the Settings icon (top right):

  • Auto (Country-Aware): Automatically sets Metric for worldwide users and Imperial for United States locations based on selected location.
  • Metric: Celsius (°C), km/h, hPa, mm, km.
  • Imperial: Fahrenheit (°F), mph, inHg, in, mi.

Home Screen Widgets

Weather Origin includes two native Android widgets:

  • 2×1 Compact Weather Widget: Quick glance at current temperature, condition, wind, and humidity.
  • 4×2 Weather & Sun Widget: Detailed forecast including solar ephemeris (sunrise, sunset, twilight) and atmospheric metrics.
