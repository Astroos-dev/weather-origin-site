Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026 · Version 1.0

Privacy Commitment: Weather Origin does not collect, sell, or monetize any personal information. The app does not use accounts, user logins, or advertising trackers.

1. No Account System & No Personal Data Collection

Weather Origin is designed to provide meteorological and astronomical intelligence without requiring user registration. We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, contacts, financial data, or device hardware IDs.

2. Location Information Usage

Weather Origin requires geographic coordinates solely to compute solar ephemerides (sunsets, twilight phases, astronomical darkness) and to fetch local atmospheric forecast data.

  • Precise Location (GPS): When location permission is granted, GPS coordinates are processed locally on your device to query meteorological data for your immediate area.
  • Manual City Search: You may search for locations manually without granting GPS access.
  • No Tracking: Location coordinates are never saved to our servers, never logged in user profiles, and never shared with third parties.

3. On-Device Local Storage

Weather Origin stores lightweight configuration and preferences directly on your device using native local storage (Capacitor Preferences):

  • Unit preferences (Metric, Imperial, or Auto).
  • Last selected location name and coordinates for immediate startup caching.
  • Latest weather snapshot for offline and home screen widget display.
  • Daily Weather Brief toggle state.

All stored data remains on your local device and is removed if you uninstall the application.

4. Third-Party Meteorological Services

To provide high-resolution weather models and geospatial queries, Weather Origin connects to trusted public meteorological APIs via secure HTTPS:

  • Open-Meteo: Provides atmospheric weather forecasts, cloud layer breakdowns, humidity, wind, and atmospheric stability metrics without requiring personal identifiers.
  • Open-Meteo Geocoding & OpenStreetMap Nominatim: Used for reverse geocoding and location search queries.
  • 7Timer!: Secondary meteorological reference model.

These network requests contain only the geographic coordinates and timezone necessary to fulfill the meteorological query. No advertising IDs or personal identifiers are attached to these network calls.

5. Notification Services

Weather Origin provides an optional Daily Weather Brief feature. When enabled:

  • Notifications are scheduled entirely on-device using standard Android notification services.
  • No external push notification servers, device tokens, or tracking beacons are used.
  • Weather Origin does not request or use exact alarm permissions (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM or USE_EXACT_ALARM).
  • You can disable notifications at any time in the app settings or Android notification settings.

6. Advertising & Data Sharing

Weather Origin is 100% ad-free. There are no advertising SDKs, no trackers, and no third-party behavioral analytics integrated into the application. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties under any circumstances.

7. Security & Children's Privacy

All network communications use industry-standard HTTPS encryption. Because Weather Origin does not collect personal data or maintain accounts, it is safe for users of all ages and does not collect information from children under 13.
